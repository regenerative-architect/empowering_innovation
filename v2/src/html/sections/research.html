Guide · self-questioning research and prompt chain

What: A deterministic, inspectable research assistant—not an online AI model. How: Select a domain, enter the actual challenge, select uncertainty and run. Inspect its working answers, competing explanations and falsification tests. Copy the prompts into a separately chosen research tool if you need updated literature. Outcome: A transparent R&D brief that distinguishes known evidence from unanswered questions.

Choose a domain and run the research engine. It can explain what is known in the embedded September 2026 snapshot; it cannot discover new papers without a connected external research workflow.
