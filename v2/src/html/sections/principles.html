OPERATING DOCTRINE

Simple experience. Serious methods.

🧭

Agency first

People choose objectives. The tool offers options, constraints and transparent assumptions rather than issuing instructions disguised as science.

🔬

Evidence ≠ outcome

Separate published findings, local observations, assumptions, simulations, and causal conclusions. Record failures and competing explanations.

🛡️

Safeguards by design

Data minimization, informed consent, equitable access, local rights, expert escalation, and a right to decline participation or data sharing.

What is this section, how should I use it, and what should happen?

What: The decision rules shared by every engine. How: Return here before adopting a proposed intervention. Outcome: A plan with explicit consent, clear evidence status, and no unsupported claim of impact.
