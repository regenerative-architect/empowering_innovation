DECISION TOOLKIT

From evidence to an accountable decision

Seven tools that keep trade-offs, uncertainty and the people affected in view. Records stay in this browser and are included in your backups.

Guide · decision toolkit

What: a brief studio, decision matrix with rank-stability test, pilot designer with a sample-size calculator, stakeholder map, risk register with an Anti-Inversion check, and a logic model. How: start with the Brief studio or load the example data from the top of the page; every tool saves locally and prints cleanly. Outcome: documents that show options, limits, who is affected and what would change your mind. Tools aid judgement; they never decide, rank people or approve anything.
