CLASSROOM MODE

Students investigate. Teachers stay in control.

Class kits, a student passport and formative review, all on this device. Students choose what to share. Nothing is ranked, scored in total or sent anywhere.

Guide · classroom

What: teachers build a class kit (question, roles, safety boundaries, rubric focus) and share it as a file; students start a project from it, keep a reflection passport and choose what to hand in; teachers import hand-ins, give formative feedback and see class-level patterns. Safety: kits contain no student data; hand-ins carry only a nickname and the entries the student ticks, after a consent tick; emails and phone numbers are refused; review is deliberately not sortable by score. Any activity involving people outside the class needs teacher approval and any required permissions. Outcome: inquiry habits without surveillance.
