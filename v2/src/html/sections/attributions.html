Scope: educational and planning software only. It cannot certify drinking water, determine benefit eligibility, issue credentials, approve electrical or structural work, guarantee outcomes, or establish causal impact on its own. Qualified practitioners and local authorities decide high-stakes matters. The Anti-Inversion check and harm-boundary filter are aids to human judgement, not guarantees.
